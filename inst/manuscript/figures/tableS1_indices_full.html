Table S1. The complete set of 22 vegetation indices computed by compute_spectral_indices(), in the canonical output order used for the exported GeoTIFF stack. Each is written as <NAME>.tif. Bands are the normalised surfaces of Section 2.3. Every ratio is evaluated through a guarded divide that returns NA where |denominator| ≤ 1 × 10−6, so a zero denominator produces no-data rather than an infinity. Indices whose bands are absent are skipped rather than written as empty layers: the last column gives the condition under which each is produced. The 5-band MicaSense RedEdge-M survey of Section 3.1 satisfies every condition and therefore yields all 22.
