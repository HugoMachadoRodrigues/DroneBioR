Table 7. Effect of the canopy-height upper-tail clip on the demonstration CHM (10,447,777 valid cells). At the default P99.5 the clip removes 52,239 cells and lowers the maximum height by 2.7 m while moving the mean by 0.15 m. Those cells are not isolated spikes: they form 169 connected patches with a median of 24 cells and a maximum of 6,172, and 50.3% of the patches span 20 cells or more, so the operation removes contiguous structure of the size and shape of emergent crowns. The clip is therefore optional (outlier_percentile = NULL), the number of cells altered is reported at run time, and the unclipped model is retained.
