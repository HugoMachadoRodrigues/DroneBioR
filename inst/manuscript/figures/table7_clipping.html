Those cells are not isolated spikes: they form 177 connected patches with a median of 15 cells and a maximum of 6,248, and 46.3% of the patches span 20 cells or more, so the operation still removes contiguous structure of the size and shape of emergent crowns. The clip is therefore optional (outlier_percentile = NULL), the number of cells altered is reported at run time, and the unclipped model is retained.
